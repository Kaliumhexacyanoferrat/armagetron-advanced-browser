How to play

Your cycle never stops, and it leaves a wall behind it. Make the others crash into a wall before you do; the last one riding wins the round.

Drive close along a wall to go faster. Rubber saves you for a moment when you drive into one. Your brake lasts a second and slowly fills up again.
